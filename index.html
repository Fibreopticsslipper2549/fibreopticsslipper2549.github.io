🤔 What is autometaresearch?

autometaresearch is a powerful, user-friendly application that brings the world of meta-research to your Windows computer. Whether you're a student, researcher, or just curious about science, this tool lets you explore and understand research studies — all powered by advanced language models.

The application takes complex research concepts and presents them in a clear, visual way. It searches through millions of scientific papers, maps out connections between studies, and even provides independent reviews — everything you need for a comprehensive understanding, right at your fingertips.

✨ Key Features That Make Research Easy

🔍 Smart Literature Search

Instantly search millions of biomedical research papers using advanced AI. The app connects to PubMed and other scientific databases to find exactly what you need.

🗺️ Visual Research Maps

See the big picture. The app creates interactive maps that show how different studies relate to each other, making complex connections easy to understand.

💻 Complete Source Code

Every study includes its full code. You can verify everything yourself — full transparency, no hidden steps. Perfect for reproducible research.

📝 Independent Reviews

Each study gets reviewed independently. You get multiple perspectives on the research, helping you understand the strengths and potential limitations.

📊 Bibliometrics Analysis

Track research impact and trends. The app provides detailed metrics on citations, publication patterns, and research output — perfect for understanding the scientific landscape.

🔄 Fully Reproducible

Everything is designed for reproducibility. You can rerun any study, verify all results, and trust that the findings are solid and transparent.
